Welcome! I am a fifth-year CS Ph.D. student at UCLA working on natural language processing (NLP), under the supervision of Professor Nanyun Peng. I've spent my summers as an intern at Google, AI2, and Amazon. From 2016 to 2020, I was an undergraduate at the Department of Automation at Tsinghua University.

My research interest lies in tasks that require deep intellect and nuanced assessment, which cannot be captured by a single metric (e.g., accuracy), including 1) controllable and creative generation; 2) machine reasoning, ideation, and problem-solving; 3) evaluation methods for these open-ended tasks; and 4) NLP + X, X = {music, agent, cognitive science, computational social science, ...}.
